Travel & where to stay

Getting here

Fly into Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KUL) — direct from London, or via the Gulf, Singapore or India.

The city is about an hour away by car, or take the KLIA Ekspres train to KL Sentral in around 30 minutes, then a short taxi ride.

Getting around

Download Grab before you land — it’s how everyone gets around KL: cheap, quick and reliable.

Before you fly

  1. Check your passport — it needs at least six months’ validity on arrival.
  2. Visas — UK passport holders don’t need one for a short visit. Travelling on another passport? Check your own rules early, and let us know if we can help.
  3. Digital Arrival Card — most visitors must complete one online within the three days before landing. It’s free on the official site, imigresen-online.imi.gov.my; ignore any lookalike that charges a fee.
